# Systematic Debugging

This skill is maintained by AIPowers. Follow the workflow below when this skill applies.

## Activation details

- AIPowers skill ID: systematic-debugging
- Priority: 90
- Triggers: debug, bug, root cause, fix failing test, unexpected error, regression

## Instructions

Do not guess or make speculative edits. Reproduce the failure and capture the exact error. Trace the failing behavior to its earliest incorrect assumption or state. Form a testable hypothesis, change one cause at a time, and rerun the reproduction. Add a regression test where practical. Verify the fix and check for nearby regressions.
